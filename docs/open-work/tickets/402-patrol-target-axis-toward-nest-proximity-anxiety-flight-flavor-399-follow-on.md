## Why

400's `protect_bias` (parenting bias lifting whole-DSE Patrol for bold +
high-temper cats with active ParentingActivity) inflates how often a
protective parent patrols, but doesn't direct where. The tiger-mother
patrols generally; she doesn't specifically circle the nest. Helicopter
scaredy parents (high anxiety + low boldness) need a *different* target
flavor — flee-paths near kittens rather than fight-positions at threats.
Without this axis, both archetypes express as inflated Patrol frequency
without the substrate-honest target shape.

## Scope

- New axis on `PatrolTargetDse`: `nest_proximity_bonus`. When actor has
  `ParentingActivity` with dependent kittens at a known nest tile cluster,
  patrol-target tiles closer to nest get a lift proportional to
  `protect_bias × inverse_distance(tile, nest_centroid)`.
- New axis: `flight_flavor_modulation`. When actor's `Personality.anxiety
  > 0.6 && Personality.boldness < 0.4`, the target-taking biases toward
  flee-path-from-nest tiles (away-from-threat AND toward-cover) rather
  than approach-to-threat tiles. Anxiety + low boldness = helicopter
  scaredy.
- L2 trace surfaces the per-target lift and the fight/flight flavor.
- Scenario test: `parenting_protect_near_nest` (bold tiger mother patrols
  nest tiles); `parenting_helicopter_flight` (anxious low-bold mother
  patrols toward flee-paths).

## Out of scope

- Whole-DSE protect_bias formula (ships in 400).
- Hunt target-axis (ticket 401; separate analogous follow-on).
- Restructuring of fight/flight at the L3 level (the flavor here is
  target-taking, not action-choice; cat still picks Patrol DSE).
