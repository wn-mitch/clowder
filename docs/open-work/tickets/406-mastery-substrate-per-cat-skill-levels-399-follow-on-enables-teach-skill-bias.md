## Why

400's `teach_skill_bias` formula references `mastery(action)` — "you
teach what you have mastered" — but no `mastery` scalar exists on cats
today. Hunt success is governed by `Personality.boldness +
Personality.diligence` + need + prey-target; there's no representation of
"this cat KNOWS Hunt well from years of practice." Without mastery, the
tiger mother teaches Hunt to her kitten in formula but has no substrate
to distinguish her teaching quality from a novice mother's.

This ticket adds the mastery substrate so 400's `teach_skill_bias`
becomes mechanically active.

## Scope

- Decision: add a `Mastery` Component (`HashMap<Action, f32>`) OR infer
  mastery from event history (lifetime success-count + age).
  Recommendation: start with inferred — cheap, no new Component, no
  serialization concern — and promote to explicit Component only if
  needed later. Inferred formula:
  `mastery(action) = sigmoid(lifetime_success_count[action] / age_seasons × scaling)`.
- Wire mastery into `teach_skill_bias` per 400's formula:
  `teach_skill_bias = mastery(action) × diligence × ambition ×
  respect_need_deficit × kitten_weaning_maturity × engagement`.
- L2 trace surfaces per-action mastery for focal-trace observability.
- `just inspect` shows per-cat mastery vector.

## Out of scope

- Skill *progression* mechanism (mastery growing through practice). The
  inferred formula naturally produces this via success_count, but no
  explicit "leveling up" event is added.
- Skill *decay* (loss of mastery over time without practice). Initial
  scope assumes monotonic.
- Per-target mastery (mastery of hunting birds vs. hunting rabbits). Use
  one mastery score per Action class.
