## Why

400 ships `ParentingActivity.relationships: Vec<RelationshipTo>` with
`ParentalKind` enum including `BondFormed`, but only declares the variant
— there's no adoption rule that produces BondFormed entries at runtime.
This ticket adds the adoption rule: accumulated bond-witness over
proximity + colony-time → threshold crossing → BondFormed RelationshipTo
inserted on the observer's ParentingActivity targeting the bonded cat.
This is the substrate for "an auntie comes to consider her niece her
daughter," "a close friend becomes parental over time" — relationships
that form through accumulated affinity, not through biology or marriage.

**Mythic-texture canary contributor.** When the threshold crosses and a
BondFormed adoption fires, it produces a named event that counts toward
the ≥1-named-event-per-sim-year canary alongside Fate-awakening /
ShadowFox-banishment / etc. Per memory
`project_bondformed_adopted_mythic_texture`: the threshold MUST be tuned
to produce events at roughly ≥1 per active relationship cluster per sim
year.

## Scope

- New Component (or field on existing relationship-tracking substrate):
  `BondWitnessAccumulator` tracking per-observer/per-target accumulated
  affinity weight from proximity + JointIntention(non-romantic)-class bonds
  + colony-time.
- Per-tick accumulation system: when observer + target in tile-range AND
  both are alive AND neither already has Biological relationship toward
  the other, increment counter by `(observer.warmth + target.warmth) /
  2 × bond_multiplier × base_rate`.
- Threshold check: when counter > `BOND_FORMED_THRESHOLD` AND observer
  doesn't already have a non-Biological RelationshipTo toward target,
  insert `RelationshipTo { target, kind: BondFormed, bond_strength: 0.5,
  parental_engagement: 0.0, partner: None, ... }` on observer's
  ParentingActivity.
- Emit a named mythic-texture event (e.g.,
  `Feature::BondFormedAdoption { observer, target, name: "..." }`) at
  threshold crossing. Naming convention: "<observer> has come to
  consider <target> their <son/daughter>".
- Tuning constants in `sim_constants.rs`: `BOND_FORMED_THRESHOLD`,
  `BOND_WITNESS_BASE_RATE`. Initial values calibrated to produce ≥1
  event per sim year per active relationship cluster (verify via soak).
