## Why

400's `provision_bias` (a parenting bias lifting whole-DSE Hunt for
diligent + loyal cats with active ParentingActivity) inflates *how often*
a provisioner cat hunts, but doesn't direct *where* that food goes. The
hard-working union man hunts more, but might hunt anywhere — there's no
substrate axis preferring prey targets that produce food for the
partner's Stores. Without this axis, the §7.M.2 spec text "diligent →
Hunt-biased" expresses as inflated frequency rather than directed
provisioning, weakening the archetype.

## Scope

- New axis on `HuntTargetDse`: `partner_stores_proximity_bonus` (or
  similar). When the actor has `ParentingActivity` with a partner whose
  Stores are at a known tile, prey targets that yield food consumable at
  partner's Stores location get a target-taking lift proportional to
  `provision_bias × inverse_distance(prey, partner_Stores)`.
- L2 trace surfaces the per-target lift so focal-trace shows the directed
  preference.
- Scenario test: diligent-low-compassion father with partner-at-Stores
  selects prey targets closer to partner over equivalent prey farther
  away (verify via `just scenario parenting_father_directs_hunt`).

## Out of scope

- Whole-DSE provision_bias formula (ships in 400).
- Patrol target-axis (ticket 402; separate analogous follow-on).
